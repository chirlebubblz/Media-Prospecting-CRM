# Media Outreach Pitch Templates: Compliance, Risk & Finance

These battle-tested templates are tailored specifically for compliance officers, legal counsels, podcast hosts, and industry editors. They avoid marketing buzzwords and focus on substance, practical lessons, and timely regulatory hooks.

---

## Template 1: The Podcast Guest Pitch (Topic Hook & Episode Tie-In)

**Use for:** Reaching out to podcast hosts and producers.  
**Subject:** Guest Idea for [Podcast Name]: [Punchy Topic Hook]

```text
Hi [Host Name],

Really enjoyed your recent episode with [Past Guest Name] on [Topic Discussed]—particularly your point regarding [Specific insight or quote from the episode]. 

Given your audience of [compliance / risk / finance leaders], I wanted to propose a timely topic for an upcoming conversation:

[Working Title / Hook: e.g., "Why 80% of Whistleblower Reports Fail Before Triage—and How Modern Risk Teams Are Fixing Them"]

A few concrete, actionable takeaways we could unpack:
• [Takeaway 1: e.g., The critical blind spots in traditional annual compliance surveys vs continuous feedback loops.]
• [Takeaway 2: e.g., How emerging regulatory expectations (SEC/DOJ) are shifting liability toward middle management.]
• [Takeaway 3: e.g., A practical 3-step audit checklist any team can run this quarter to test ethical reporting culture.]

By way of brief background: I lead [Ethics Insight / Your Role], focusing on [1-sentence summary of your expertise/mission]. I’ve spent the last [X years] helping organizations [key result or practical experience].

Would this angle resonate with your listeners for an upcoming episode? Either way, keep up the fantastic work on the show.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title / Ethics Insight]
[Your LinkedIn Profile URL]
[Your Website / One-Sheet Link]
```

## Template 2: The Publication / Op-Ed Contributor Pitch (Blogs & Trade Media)

**Use for:** Editors at *Corporate Compliance Insights*, *SCCE Blog*, *Insurance Thought Leadership*, or industry journals.  
**Subject:** Guest Article Pitch: [Proposed Main Title]

```text
Hi [Editor Name],

I am a regular reader of [Publication Name] and especially appreciated [Author Name]’s recent piece on [Recent Article Topic].

I would love to contribute a practical, vendor-neutral article for [Publication Name]’s readership of compliance and risk executives. 

Here are three proposed headline angles based on current industry developments:

1. [Headline 1: e.g., The Hidden Gaps in Whistleblower Protection: What 2026 Enforcement Actions Reveal]
2. [Headline 2: e.g., Moving From 'Check-the-Box' to Behavioral Compliance: 4 Metrics Boards Actually Care About]
3. [Headline 3: e.g., Automated Risk Audits: How to Safeguard Internal Investigations Without Compromising Trust]

Abstract (Option 1):
[2-3 sentences explaining the problem, the core insight, and the practical takeaways the reader will walk away with. E.g., Many compliance programs rely on legacy annual attestations that fail to flag micro-culture toxicity. This article breaks down how forward-thinking GRC teams are adopting psychological safety metrics to catch ethical lapses months before they trigger regulatory inquiries.]

Article specs:
• Word count: ~1,200–1,500 words (formatted according to your editorial style)
• 100% original, data-supported, and vendor-neutral
• Ready for submission within [X] days of outline approval

My bio and credentials can be viewed here: [LinkedIn Link / Brief Bio].

Would any of these three angles fit your upcoming editorial calendar?

Warm regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title / Ethics Insight]
[Your Phone / Contact Info]
```

## Template 3: The "Breaking Regulatory Action" News Peg Pitch

**Use for:** Pitching commentary when the DOJ, SEC, FCA, or a major regulator drops new guidelines or penalty fines.  
**Subject:** Commentary / Analysis: [Regulator]’s New [Rule/Action] on [Issue]

```text
Hi [Host / Editor Name],

Following [Regulator's name]'s announcement this morning regarding [Specific enforcement action / new compliance guidance], many risk and compliance teams are wondering how to adapt their internal controls before the deadline.

If you are planning an episode or article examining what this means in practice, I’d be glad to share quick practitioner analysis on:

1. The immediate compliance implications for [finance / corporate] organizations.
2. The specific mistake companies will make trying to comply with [Guideline name].
3. What CCOs and General Counsels should instruct their teams to audit this week.

Happy to provide a brief 800-word breakdown, an audio soundbite, or jump on a 20-minute recording.

Let me know if this would be helpful for your audience!

Best,

[Your Name]
[Your Title, Ethics Insight]
[Direct Phone / WhatsApp]
```

## Template 4: The Strategic Follow-Up Sequence

### Follow-Up 1: The Value-Add Nudge (Send 5–7 business days after Initial Pitch)
**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject Line]

```text
Hi [Host / Editor Name],

Following up briefly on my note below regarding [brief mention of the topic hook]. 

I recently noticed [relevant news item, data point, or new case study], which underscores why this issue is becoming top of mind for compliance teams right now.

Would you be open to a 10-minute introductory call or reviewing a quick 3-bullet outline?

Thanks again for your time and editorial leadership!

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up 2: The Graceful Close (Send 7–10 days after Follow-Up 1)
**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject Line]

```text
Hi [Host / Editor Name],

I know your inbox is constantly flooded with guest and story pitches, so I will assume the timing isn't right for [Podcast / Publication Name] at the moment. 

I’ll keep tuning in to [Podcast / Publication Name], and if a future news hook or regulatory change makes this topic relevant down the road, I’d love to reconnect.

Wishing you continued success with the platform!

Best regards,

[Your Name]
```
