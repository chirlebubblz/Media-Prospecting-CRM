### 2. "Beyond the Check-the-Box: Operationalizing Behavioral Risk"
* Why conventional annual compliance training does not stop fraud or ethical breaches.
* How risk leaders can measure ethical sentiment in real-time.

### 3. "The Next Regulatory Wave: AI Governance & Compliance Architecture"
* The emerging compliance risks of adopting generative AI in financial decision-making.
* How compliance teams can audit black-box models and automated workflows.
* Crafting defensible compliance audit trails for regulatory scrutiny.

* Immediate steps to take when a credible allegation of financial misconduct emerges.
