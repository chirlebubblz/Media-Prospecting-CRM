## 3. High-Conversion Pitching Best Practices in YMYL / Compliance

1. **Focus on Concrete Lessons, Not Sales:** Compliance and risk editors reject sales pitches immediately. Always provide vendor-neutral frameworks, actionable steps, or breakdowns of recent regulatory actions (DOJ, SEC, FCA, FINRA).
2. **Propose 3 Titles + 1 Abstract:** Editors love options. Send 3 punchy, specific headline ideas accompanied by a 2-3 sentence abstract of what readers/listeners will learn.
3. **Reference a Specific Past Episode / Article:** Mentioning a past guest or recent episode proves you aren't blasting spam and immediately puts you in the top 5% of pitches.
