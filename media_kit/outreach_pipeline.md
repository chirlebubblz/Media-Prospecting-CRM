# Media Outreach Pipeline & CRM

Track your outreach progress across podcasts, blogs, and trade publications.

### Status Definitions:
* 🔍 **Researching**: Target identified; researching recent episodes, editor names, and specific angles.
* 📨 **Pitched**: First pitch sent; awaiting response.
* ⏳ **Follow-up 1 / 2**: Nudge sent after 5-7 business days.
* 🎙️ **Booked / Accepted**: Interview scheduled or article outline accepted.
* 🚀 **Published / Live**: Episode or article is live.
* 🛑 **Declined / Unresponsive**: Passed or no reply after full cadence.
